About Dr. Badie Idris

Dr. Badie Idris is an internationally recognized consultant aesthetic surgeon with more than 17 years of experience in advanced cosmetic and regenerative treatments, as stated on his official website.

His profile highlights an MBBS and MS in surgery, a Ph.D. in Aesthetics (USA), and membership in the American Society of Regenerative Medicine. His work encompasses aesthetic procedures and regenerative approaches.

His philosophy emphasizes individualized care, professional expertise and treatment plans that reflect each patient's needs. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss goals, suitable options, benefits and possible risks.
